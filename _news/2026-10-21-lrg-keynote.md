Keynote at the [Learning and Reasoning with Graphs (LRG) workshop](https://lrg-workshop.github.io/)
at BNAIC/BeNeLearn 2026 in Maastricht: "The Decision Nobody Made: A Case for
Explicit Knowledge Models". Explicit knowledge models record human decisions
about what data means and make those decisions usable in computation. Without
them, an LLM makes these decisions on its own.
