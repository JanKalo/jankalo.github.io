New vision paper at AKBC 2026 in Budapest: [Operationalization Is Part of the
Claim](/publications/#kalo2026operationalization). Whether the UK or Australia
emitted more CO₂ in 2022 depends on how emissions are counted. The paper argues
that generated answers should keep track of how each value was defined and
estimated. [Paper](https://aclanthology.org/2026.akbc-1.20/) and
[poster](/assets/pdf/kalo2026operationalization-poster.pdf).
